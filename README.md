# Cupiddesk: What if Cupids had jobs?

A fictional single-player parody game that runs in your browser. You switch on Cupid OS, clock in at the Cupiddesk, and handle relationship problems that pigeons drop into your queue as LoveNotes, before their Heartbreak Timers run out.

> **Parody notice:** Cupiddesk is a fictional parody game created for entertainment and humor. Its characters, scenarios, conversations, and resolutions are fictional and are not intended to provide real relationship, psychological, legal, medical, or professional advice. Do not use the game's fictional responses as guidance for real-world decisions. Cupiddesk is inspired by a day in the life of a service desk job and the general ITIL way of working. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to ServiceNow, Jira or any other product, and no real screens are copied.

> **AI disclosure:** The code of this game was made with the help of generative AI.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser (double-click it, or use VS Code's "Live Server"). No install, no build, no internet needed. Progress is saved only in your browser (localStorage).

## How it plays

- **LoveNotes** are generated by the system with a prefix and number:
  - `LNI0001041` = **Incident** (something went wrong: a fight, a forgotten anniversary)
  - `LNR0001042` = **Request** (someone wants something: a cake, a serenade, or something impossible)
- **Pick up** a LoveNote to start its **Heartbreak Timer**. Near-break notes have the least time. Cupiddesk uses its own heart scale (Minor, Cracking, Near-break) instead of P1/P2/P3.
- **Ask for details**, then pick replies that fit the problem. Replies that match the hidden category raise **Calm** the most. Bad replies make it worse.
- **Close it**: Patch (45% Calm), Mend (75% Calm, plus a speed bonus), Escalate to automated **Cupid L2**, send a request to **L2 Fulfilment**, or **Decline** impossible requests (love potions, cloning, "send me a girlfriend").
- **Triage**: set the Category and Heart condition before closing for bonus points.
- Every shift ends with a **report card** (S, A, B, C or D).
- Keys **1 to 4** send replies, **D** asks for details.

## Story mode

Five story shifts (chapters) that unlock features step by step, with mails from Senior Cupid, Heart Resources, L2 Fulfilment and a mysterious "???". After that, endless **Overtime** shifts get busier each time.

The story in `js/story.js` is a stand-in. Rewrite the shift titles, intros, mails and scripted story LoveNotes with your own plot.

## Desktop apps

Cupiddesk · Mailbox · Heart status (donut chart) · Cloudy Search browser · Trash · About. Windows can be dragged and Cupiddesk can be maximised. Works on phones too.

## Files

- `index.html`: page shell
- `css/styles.css`: Cupid OS theme (light and dark mode)
- `js/data.js`: LoveNote templates, names, replies, points and settings
- `js/story.js`: story shifts and mails (write your story here)
- `js/game.js`: game logic, timers and saved state
- `js/ui.js`: desktop, windows, screens, sounds
- `CREDITS.md`: credits
